    Posting a video

    Get the video onto your phone first — Google Drive, WhatsApp (send it as a document, or the quality drops), or a USB cable.

    1. Open TikTok → tap + at the bottom → tap Upload on the right.
    2. Select your video → Next. Don’t trim it — it’s already 9:16 and the right length.
    3. Write your caption. The first line is the hook — that’s all that shows in the feed.
    4. Most important step: tap Add link → Product and tag the product from your shop. Skip this and the video earns views but cannot earn a single sale.
    5. Choose a cover. Don’t accept the first frame — pick the frame where the product reads clearly, and add text to the cover.
    6. Tap Post, then reply to comments in the first hour. TikTok weighs early engagement heavily.

    Posting a photo carousel

    Carousels often beat video for home decor — people swipe, and every swipe counts as watch time. You already have good product photography for this.

    1. TikTok → + → Upload → switch to the Photo tab at the top.
    2. Select your photos. The limit is 35, but 6–10 performs best. Order matters — photo one is your hook.
    3. Put a text overlay on photo one (something like “the desk set that’s actually handmade”). Keep the rest as clean product shots.
    4. Pick a trending sound, not your own audio.
    5. Caption plus product tag — same rule as video, no tag means no sale.
    6. Post. Carousels take 24–48 hours to pick up distribution, slower than video.

    Optimizing a listing

    In Seller Center: seller-us.tiktok.com → Products → Manage Products → Edit.

    1. Title: run it through Tab 1. The product type belongs in the first three words.
    2. Set an original price. None of your 35 products has a compare-at price right now, so no discount badge shows anywhere in your store. Set it 30–40% above your selling price. It is free and it is the fastest lever you have.
    3. Image 1: white background, whole product visible. Images 2–3: lifestyle — on a real desk, a real table. Image 4: a dimensions graphic. Image 5: close-up of the print.
    4. Description: short bullets. Size, material, care instructions, and one line saying “hand block printed — every piece varies slightly.” That variation is your differentiator, not a defect.
    5. Check stock. A sold-out variant leaves the listing live but blocks the sale — this may be exactly what’s happening with the spa towel.
    6. Shipping: conversion drops sharply past a 5-day delivery estimate. Get it as low as you can.
